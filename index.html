Phoenix Nights Now Are Warming Faster Than Days

Phoenix has always been infamous for the hot days it has. However the greatest change in its weather records actually happens to be during the night time. According to the NDAA's yearly summaries, the average daily high and the average daily low for every year have both rose. However through 1966 and 2025, they have not risen at the same rate. Night time is when people finally can get a chance to cool off, its incredibly important in as hot of a place as Arizona. This story groups these 60 years into six 10 year periods that compare the first period with teh most recent to answer the question of, has Phoenix's days, or nights risen more.

D1 · Story Frame

Claim:Its nights. From 1966 to 1975 and 2016 to 2025, Phoenix's average daily low rose 8 fahrenheit, while the average daily high rose 3.2 fahrenheit. The nights warmed more than double the days.

Year to year, the averages of each fall and rise. However over six decades the low has began to climb faster. With each reaching hteir highest values in this 60 year record during 2024. 90 fahrenheit for the average high and 66.6 fahrenheit for the average low. In 1966, the average high was 30.3 fahrenheit warmer than the average low. And by 2025, the gap was 23.3 fahrenheit.
